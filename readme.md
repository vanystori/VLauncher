# VLAUNCHER

## WEB EDITION RELEASE ANNOUNCEMENT

### ONE LIBRARY. ONE COMMUNITY. ONE LAUNCHER.

**Vany's Studio is proud to introduce VLauncher!**

A gaming platform designed to bring your gaming library, digital store, friends, messaging and community together under one roof.

Discover games, manage your collection, connect with friends, chat with other players and establish your own identity within a growing gaming community.

Whether you're looking for your next game, organising your collection or catching up with friends, **VLauncher** brings these experiences together in one convenient platform.

Welcome to **VLauncher**.

# DISCOVER THE PLATFORM

## YOUR PERSONAL GAME LIBRARY

Your games deserve a home.

The **VLauncher Game Library** gives you a central place to organise and access your gaming collection.

Keep your games together, browse your collection and enjoy a more convenient way to manage your gaming experience.

## THE VLAUNCHER STORE

Discover something new.

Browse available games, find something that interests you and expand your collection through the **VLauncher Store**.

## INTEGRATED GAME DOWNLOADS

Getting your games should be simple.

Download games directly through **VLauncher** with its integrated downloading experience.

Your library and downloads come together in one place.
